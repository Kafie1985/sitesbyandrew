# Sites By Andrew design direction

## Brand
- Business: Sites By Andrew
- Audience: small local businesses, especially cleaning companies
- Offer: clear, credible websites and practical small-business solutions
- Tone: warm, direct, capable, never agency-jargon-heavy

## Visual system
- Deep navy ink and blue accents, softened paper backgrounds, and a small citrus accent
- Bricolage Grotesque display type with Nunito Sans body text
- Generous whitespace, bold readable type, rounded details used sparingly
- Mobile-first layouts with accessible contrast and obvious actions
